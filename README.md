# BookFinish 📚

A landing page for BookFinish - an accountability app that helps people finish the books they buy using financial commitment and streak tracking.

## The Problem

People buy books with good intentions but never finish reading them. BookFinish solves this by adding financial accountability and gamification to the reading experience.

## How It Works

- Lock up money when you commit to finishing a book
- Set your own deadline
- Finish on time? Get your money back + bonus from people who quit
- Don't finish? Your money goes to people who completed their books
- Build reading streaks like Duolingo
